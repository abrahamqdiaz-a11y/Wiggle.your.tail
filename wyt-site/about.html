Our Story

Built by Someone Who
Understands Pets

Animals have always been a part of Christa's life. Growing up around dogs, cats, small animals, and reptiles created a lifelong connection to animal care that eventually became a professional career in 2019.

Wiggle Your Tail was created after seeing how difficult it was for pet owners to find care that felt reliable, consistent, and personal. Too many services felt rushed or treated pet care like a side job.

This business was built differently. Today, Wiggle Your Tail is a bonded and insured, BBB accredited pet care provider in Sarasota, and the Best of SRQ Platinum winner for 2025. But the awards matter less than the work behind them: structured, professional care focused on routine, communication, safety, and genuine attention to each animal's personality and needs.
