Common Questions

Dog Training
FAQ

Does Wiggle Your Tail offer dog training in Sarasota?
Wiggle Your Tail provides structured behavioral support and training reinforcement as part of our dog care services. Our experienced team works with dogs of all ages — from puppies learning basic commands to adult dogs working through behavioral challenges.
What methods do you use for dog training?
We use positive reinforcement-based methods focused on reward, consistency, and patience. We work with your dog's personality and existing routine rather than using punishment or force. Our approach builds a calm, confident dog that responds reliably.
Can you help with leash pulling?
Yes. Leash manners and pulling are among the most common issues we work on during structured walks. Sarasota's sidewalks, parks, and trails provide excellent environments for reinforcing loose-leash walking alongside real-world distractions.
Do you work with puppies?
Yes. We love working with puppies and are experienced with the critical socialization window (typically 8–16 weeks). Early consistency, positive exposure to new environments, and gentle routine-building all set puppies up for long-term success.
Can you work alongside my existing dog trainer in Sarasota?
Absolutely. Many of our clients work with a separate professional trainer for formal sessions and rely on us to reinforce the training between appointments. We'll review your trainer's instructions and follow them consistently during every visit.
How do I get started?
Contact us to schedule a free meet and greet. We'll learn about your dog's history, behavior goals, and current routine, then recommend the right combination of services to support your training objectives. There's no commitment required.
