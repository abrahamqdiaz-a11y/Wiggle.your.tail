Cat Sitting Sarasota

Care Built Around
Your Cat's Routine

Most cats are calmer staying where everything already smells, sounds and feels familiar. No carrier, no car ride, no unfamiliar boarding room — just their own windows, their own furniture and their own schedule.

Hiring a cat sitter in Sarasota should get you more than someone stopping by to top up a bowl. A Wiggle Your Tail visit is professional in-home care: your cat is looked after by a bonded and insured pet-care professional who works to written instructions, checks on how your cat is doing, and reports back to you afterwards.

Every visit is built around the individual cat: their feeding schedule, their personality, how much play and company they want, and any care instructions you leave with us. A confident cat who greets the sitter at the door and a nervous cat who watches from the top of the stairs get very different visits — by design.

What's Included in a
Cat-Sitting Visit?

Visits run 30, 45 or 60 minutes. What happens inside that time is set by you at the Meet & Greet — here's what a typical cat visit covers.

Feeding to Your Instructions
Wet food, dry food, portioned meals or a timed schedule — we feed exactly the way you've written it down, and note what your cat actually ate.
Fresh Water & Bowls
Water refreshed and bowls rinsed at every visit, plus a check on fountains or multiple water stations if that's part of your cat's setup.
Litter Care
Boxes scooped and the area around them tidied, following your instructions on litter type and how you'd like waste disposed of.
Playtime & Enrichment
Wand toys, chase games or a window seat and a quiet lap — as much or as little activity as your cat actually enjoys.
Company for Social Cats
Cats who want the attention get it — brushing, conversation and a warm lap so the house doesn't feel empty for days on end.
Patient Care for Shy Cats
Nervous cats are never dragged out from a hiding spot. Food, water and litter are handled calmly, and interaction happens on their terms.
Medication Administration
Available as long as your pet doesn't pose a threat. Tell us your cat's needs upfront so we assign the right sitter — additional fees apply for injections or complex routines.
Photos & Visit Report
A pet report card and photos land in the Time to Pet app after every visit, and you can message your sitter directly from there.
Cats Stay Indoors
For safety, we keep cats indoors for the duration of our visits — including cats who normally have patio or backyard access at home.

Kittens, adults and senior cats, single-cat homes and multi-cat households all get the same approach: the routine you already use, followed the way you use it. Need the house looked after as well — mail, packages, plants and regular home checks? That's covered by our snowbird & house sitting service, which can run alongside your cat visits.

How often should a cat sitter visit my cat?
Most cat owners book at least one visit per day so food, water and the litter box are handled on a consistent schedule. Two visits a day suit cats on medication, multi-cat households, kittens, and cats who are used to a lot of company. We'll talk through your cat's routine at the free Meet & Greet and recommend a schedule that fits.
Can you care for shy or anxious cats?
Yes. Shy cats are given a low-pressure visit: food, water and litter are handled quietly, and we let your cat decide how much interaction they want rather than pulling them out from under the bed. Tell us where your cat hides and what settles them, and we'll follow that at every visit.
Do you clean the litter box during each visit?
Yes. Litter care is part of your cat's visit routine — scooping the box and tidying the area around it. Let us know at the Meet & Greet where the boxes are, which litter you use, and how you'd like waste disposed of, and we'll follow your instructions every time.
Can you administer cat medication?
Yes, as long as your pet doesn't pose a threat. Let us know your cat's needs upfront so we can assign the right sitter. Additional fees apply for injections or complex routines.
Will I receive photos and updates?
Yes. Every visit includes photos and a pet report card sent through our Time to Pet client app, so you can see how your cat is doing while you're away. You can also message your sitter directly through the app.
Do you care for multiple cats?
Yes. Multi-cat households are welcome, including homes where cats are fed separately or have their own routines. Drop-in and Travel Care visits are priced per visit with an additional pet fee of $5 per visit.
Is a Meet & Greet required before the first visit?
It isn't strictly required — with clear instructions and access to your home we can start right away — but we highly recommend and prefer a free Meet & Greet with all new clients. It's the best way to assure the safety of your pets and our pet care professionals, and it's how we learn your cat's routine.
What areas around Sarasota do you serve?
We serve Sarasota, Downtown Sarasota, Fruitville, Siesta Key, Gulf Gate Estates, Bee Ridge, Bradenton, Longboat Key, Lakewood Ranch, Osprey and surrounding areas. Please note there are additional fees for locations outside of our standard service areas — send us your address and we'll confirm availability.
