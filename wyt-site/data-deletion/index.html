Data Deletion Request

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data held by Wiggle Your Tail Pet Care. We are committed to honoring these requests promptly and transparently.

What Data We Hold

Wiggle Your Tail Pet Care may hold personal data you provided when contacting us, booking services, or creating an account in our Time to Pet client portal. This may include your name, email address, phone number, home address, and information about your pets.

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of your personal data, please contact us using one of the following methods:

By Email

Send your request to contact@wiggleyourtail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Please include your full name and the email address associated with your account so we can locate your records.

By Contact Form

Use our contact form and mention "Data Deletion Request" in your message. Include your name and email so we can identify your records.

What Happens Next

We will confirm receipt of your request within 5 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days. We will notify you once your data has been removed. Please note that we may be required to retain certain records for legal, tax, or compliance purposes — we will let you know if any data must be kept and explain why.

Third-Party Platforms

If you have an account on our Time to Pet scheduling platform, you will need to also request deletion directly through Time to Pet, as that data is managed by their systems. Please visit timetopet.com for their privacy and data deletion options.

Questions

If you have any questions about this process or your data rights, please email us at contact@wiggleyourtail.com or call (941) 280-5156. We're happy to help.

Have a Question About Your Data?

We take your privacy seriously. Contact us directly and we'll respond within one business day.
