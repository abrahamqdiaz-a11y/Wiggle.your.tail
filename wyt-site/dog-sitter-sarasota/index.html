Common Questions

Dog Sitter Sarasota
FAQ

What does a dog sitter in Sarasota do?
A professional dog sitter visits your home to care for your dog while you're away — providing feeding, potty breaks, playtime, walks, and companionship. At Wiggle Your Tail, every visit includes photo updates and a pet report card so you always know how your dog is doing.
Is in-home dog sitting better than boarding in Sarasota?
For most dogs, yes. In-home sitting keeps your dog in a familiar, stress-free environment without exposure to illness, fleas, or aggression from other animals. Your dog maintains their normal routine, sleeps in their own space, and receives one-on-one attention.
How much does a dog sitter cost in Sarasota?
Drop-in visits start at $42 for 30 minutes. Overnight sitting rates are available upon request. All visits include photos, updates, and a pet report card. See our full rates on the pet sitting page.
Do you offer overnight dog sitting in Sarasota?
Yes. We offer full overnight stays from 9:30 pm to 7 am in your home. Your sitter follows your dog's evening routine — dinner, walk, cuddles — then provides breakfast and a morning walk before leaving. Additional pet fees apply.
Will my dog always have the same sitter?
We believe in dedicated sitters your dog knows and trusts. We do our best to keep the same sitter for every booking, but can assign a trained backup when needed. Booking in advance greatly helps with consistency.
How do I get started with a dog sitter in Sarasota?
Contact us to schedule a free meet and greet. We'll learn about your dog's routine, personality, and any special needs, then match you with the right sitter. There's no commitment required.
